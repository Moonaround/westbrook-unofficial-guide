Westbrook Off-Campus Housing: Move-In and Move-Out Survival Guide
Source: Westbrook University Off-Campus Student Services (unofficial student-compiled guide)

---

MOVE-IN DAY CHECKLIST

Before you put anything in the apartment, document everything.

Step 1 — Photo documentation (take timestamped photos of all of the following):
- Every wall in every room (get the corners)
- All floors and carpet — note any stains, burns, or worn areas
- All appliances (open the fridge, oven, dishwasher — photograph inside)
- All fixtures (faucets, showerhead, toilet, light switches)
- All windows (note any cracks, broken latches, gaps in seals)
- Any existing damage to doors, door frames, closets
- The mailbox and any common areas assigned to you

Step 2 — Email your photos to yourself AND to the landlord on move-in day. Having an emailed record with a timestamp is far stronger than photos that could theoretically be taken later.

Step 3 — Request a move-in condition form (or make your own) and get the landlord to sign it noting any pre-existing damage. If the landlord won't sign, send them an email listing the pre-existing damage and keep their non-response as your record.

UTILITIES: WHAT TO SET UP BEFORE YOU MOVE IN

Things to set up 1-2 weeks before your move-in date:
- Electric service (Westbrook Electric Cooperative — call (555) 300-1200 or set up online)
- Gas service if applicable (Westbrook Gas — call (555) 300-4500)
- Internet (Xfinity serves most areas near campus, 1-2 week lead time for installation)
- Renter's insurance — required by some landlords, smart regardless. Cost: $10-20/month for basic coverage through Lemonade, State Farm, or similar. Covers theft, fire, and liability.

Note: many apartments near Westbrook include heat and/or water in rent. Confirm this in writing before assuming.

---

WHAT'S ACTUALLY REQUIRED FOR WESTBROOK AREA APARTMENTS

Under Westbrook city housing code, landlords are required to provide:
- Working heat capable of maintaining 68°F minimum during October-April
- Hot water at all times
- Smoke detectors on every floor and in every bedroom
- Carbon monoxide detectors if gas appliances are present
- Secure locks on all entry doors and windows
- No pest infestations (mice, cockroaches, bedbugs)
- No mold that affects habitability
- Functioning plumbing and electrical systems

If any of these are not present or stop working, the landlord is legally required to address them promptly. If they don't, contact the Westbrook City Housing Inspection Office at housing@westbrook.gov.

MOVE-OUT: HOW TO GET YOUR DEPOSIT BACK

The most common deposit deduction traps near Westbrook:
- "Professional cleaning fee" ($100-250): Many landlords in Westbrook charge this automatically. Check your lease — some require it regardless; others only charge if the apartment is not "broom clean." If your lease doesn't require professional cleaning, a thorough cleaning by you should be sufficient.
- Wall painting/repainting: If you painted without permission, you'll be charged. Even if you painted with permission, confirm in writing whether you need to repaint to original color at move-out.
- Carpet replacement: Normal wear over years is landlord's responsibility. Stains and burns are yours. Document carpet condition at move-in.
- "Admin fees" and "processing fees": Some Westbrook landlords charge these at move-out without clear lease basis. If a fee isn't in your lease, you can dispute it.

Move-out steps:
1. Get the move-out inspection checklist from your landlord at least 2 weeks before your move-out date
2. Clean thoroughly — oven, fridge (empty and cleaned), bathroom grout, light fixtures
3. Remove all personal items and patch nail holes with spackle (available at any hardware store)
4. Do a walkthrough with the landlord PRESENT before handing over keys
5. Under Westbrook law, landlords must return deposit within 30 days with itemized deductions

If your deposit is withheld unfairly: Contact the Westbrook Tenant Rights Organization at tenants@westbrookrights.org. They offer free consultations and dispute assistance.
