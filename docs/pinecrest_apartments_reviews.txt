Westbrook University Off-Campus Housing: Pinecrest Student Apartments Reviews
Source: Westbrook University Reddit (r/WestbrookU) and student housing forums

---

Review by user pinecrest_honest_review:
Pinecrest is marketed as student housing and that's exactly what it is — affordable and functional, not fancy. $720/month for a 1BR, $980 for a 2BR. It's about a 12-minute walk to campus. What you get: older appliances (the fridges are small, the dishwasher is older but works), carpet throughout (not ideal), and thin walls. What makes it good: management (University Housing Partners) actually tries. Maintenance requests are handled within 2-3 days usually. I've had two maintenance issues in a year — a running toilet and a broken window latch — both fixed within 48 hours. Heat and water included in rent.

Review by user wbu_freshman_offcampus:
Moved off campus after freshman year to Pinecrest and don't regret it. My main advice: request a unit in the newer wing (Building D or E, built 2018). These have updated kitchens, better insulation, and USB outlets which sounds small but matters. The older buildings (A, B, C) are noticeably worse. The building has a study lounge on each floor which is actually useful for studying when my roommate is home. No gym but the university gym is a 12-minute walk. Parking is $60/month. Package delivery system (Amazon lockers in the lobby) is great, no stolen packages.

Review by user pinecrest_noise_issues:
I need to warn people: Pinecrest is marketed as "student housing" and the culture reflects that on weekends. Thursday through Saturday nights are loud. I've called the front desk about noise multiple times and they say they'll send someone but nothing really changes. If you need quiet on weekends for studying or sleeping, this is not the place. Conversely, if you want to be around other students and don't mind the social atmosphere, it's actually a fun place to live. The social events management organizes (a few per semester) are a nice touch.

Review by user pinecrest_2br_resident:
Sharing a 2BR at Pinecrest with my roommate at $490 each. It's genuinely the best value near campus if you have a roommate. The 2BR layout has a shared living room, two separate bedrooms with decent-sized closets, and a full bath. My only real complaint is the bathroom ventilation — there's a fan but it's weak and the bathroom gets humid after showers. Minor mold appeared near the ceiling after 6 months, I bleached it and it hasn't come back. Report any mold to management in writing even if you clean it yourself, for documentation purposes.

Review by user wbu_housing_comparison_2024:
I toured Pinecrest, Maplewood, and Riverside before choosing. Here's my take: Pinecrest is best for students who want to be around other students and can handle weekend noise. Maplewood is the sweet spot for location (closest to campus) and is quieter but management is inconsistent. Riverside is best for value and quiet but the bus situation is a real constraint if you don't have a car. I went with Pinecrest for the community aspect and haven't regretted it, though I wear earplugs on weekends.
