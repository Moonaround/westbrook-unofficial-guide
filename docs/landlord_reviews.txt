Westbrook Area Landlord & Property Management Reviews
Source: Westbrook Tenant Reviews Forum and Rate My Landlord (westbrook region)

---

Greenfield Property Group (manages Maplewood Apartments)
Overall rating: 2.8/5 from 47 reviews

Review: "Slow to respond to non-emergency maintenance, but they do eventually fix things. Never had a serious safety issue go unaddressed. The problem is they treat deposit deductions as a revenue stream — I've never heard of anyone getting their full deposit back. Take photos of everything." — former Maplewood tenant

Review: "Greenfield has a reputation on campus for being difficult at move-out. The 'professional cleaning fee' they charge ($150-250) seems to be charged regardless of how clean you leave the place. Check your lease — you may have the right to do your own cleaning before move-out." — WBU student housing blog

Review: "Called them about a broken heater in January, they had someone there the next day. So emergencies are handled well. But I've been waiting 6 weeks for them to replace a broken closet door. It's all about priority." — current Maplewood tenant

---

Cedar Living LLC (manages The Lofts on Cedar)
Overall rating: 4.3/5 from 89 reviews

Review: "Best property management company I've dealt with in the Westbrook area. They have an actual 24/7 emergency line that goes to a real person, not a voicemail. Every maintenance request I submitted was acknowledged within 4 hours and fixed within 24-48 hours. Deposit return was full and processed within 2 weeks of move-out." — former Lofts tenant

Review: "Cedar Living is professional and responsive but they know they're the premium option and price accordingly. Rent increases at renewal are real — factor in 5-10% per year. If you're on a budget and thinking this will stay affordable, plan for that." — Lofts on Cedar resident

Review: "The one complaint I have about Cedar Living is communication when something affects the whole building — elevator was broken for 5 days and we got zero proactive communication, had to keep asking for updates. Individual unit issues are handled great, building-wide issues less so." — Lofts resident

---

Castillo Properties (manages Riverside Commons)
Overall rating: 4.0/5 from 34 reviews

Review: "Small family operation, which means when something goes wrong you talk to an actual person who has authority to fix it. Contrast with large companies where you call a call center. My experience has been consistently positive. They treat long-term tenants well — no rent increase in year two for me." — Riverside Commons tenant

Review: "Castillo is good but has limited staff. If there's a maintenance emergency on a weekend, response time can be 24-36 hours because they don't have weekend staff on call. For minor issues this is fine; for emergencies (burst pipe, heat outage in winter) this is a problem." — Riverside resident

---

University Housing Partners (manages Pinecrest Apartments)
Overall rating: 3.5/5 from 61 reviews

Review: "They actually organize community events, which is unusual for a landlord. Maintenance is reliable if not fast (2-3 day average). The management team clearly knows they're managing student housing and sets expectations accordingly. Not trying to be luxury, just trying to be functional." — Pinecrest tenant

Review: "Mixed experience. Normal maintenance: fine. Move-out process: be very careful. They have a detailed move-out checklist that they apply strictly. I was charged $80 for not cleaning the oven. The charge was legitimate but I wasn't aware how detailed the inspection would be. Get a copy of the checklist before you clean and use it as your guide." — former Pinecrest tenant
