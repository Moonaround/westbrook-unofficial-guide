Westbrook University Off-Campus Housing: Riverside Commons Reviews
Source: Westbrook Off-Campus Housing Facebook Group

---

Review by user wbu_senior_2024:
Riverside Commons gets overlooked because it's farther from campus (20 min walk or 10 min on the Route 7 bus) but honestly it's the best value near Westbrook. $750/month for a 1BR, $1,050 for a 2BR. Utilities are NOT included (budget ~$80-100/month extra), but even with that it's cheaper than anywhere else. The complex has a pool (open May-September), a small gym, and free parking. The apartments are older — mine was last renovated in 2015 — but everything works. Management is small (family-owned, Castillo Properties) and very responsive. My stove broke and they replaced it the next day.

Review by user riverside_experience_2023:
Important things about Riverside Commons: the bus situation is key. Route 7 runs every 20 minutes during the day but drops to every 45 minutes after 9pm and stops entirely at midnight. If you don't have a car and work late in the library you will be walking 20 min home in the dark. The walk itself is fine, it's a safe neighborhood, but factor that into your decision. The pool is great in summer, I used it constantly May-August. Neighbors are a mix of students and non-students (families, young professionals) which actually makes it quieter than all-student buildings. Lease is flexible: they offer 9-month, 12-month, and month-to-month options.

Review by user wbu_international_student:
I chose Riverside Commons specifically because of the flexible lease — I'm on an international student visa and wasn't sure how long I'd need housing, so the month-to-month option was important. Month-to-month is $100/month more than the 12-month lease but worth the flexibility. The office staff was helpful with my questions and patient with my paperwork. The apartment is clean and functional. Heat is electric baseboard which can get expensive in winter — my worst month was $140 in January. Budget accordingly. The complex is very quiet and multicultural, I feel comfortable here.

Review by user riverside_mold_warning:
Posting this as a warning: I had a mold problem in my bathroom at Riverside Commons. It started small and when I reported it they initially just gave me a can of Tilex and said to clean it myself. It took me escalating to the city housing inspector to get them to actually remediate it properly. Once I involved the inspector they were responsive and paid for professional remediation. BUT: you shouldn't have to involve a city inspector to get a mold problem taken seriously. Lesson: document everything, escalate in writing, and know that you can contact the Westbrook city housing inspection office (housing@westbrook.gov) if management is unresponsive.

Review by user castillo_properties_tenant:
Castillo Properties (Riverside Commons management) is genuinely good in my experience. My lease renewal was straightforward, they didn't raise my rent at all after year one, and they were transparent about the renewal process months in advance. Deposit return was full minus a legitimate $50 cleaning fee, which I agreed with. The only consistent complaint I have is the laundry room: 6 washers for a 120-unit complex is not enough, there are always waits on Sunday evenings. Also the parking lot lights are dim, they've been asked to upgrade them and haven't. Overall I'd recommend Riverside for budget-conscious students willing to deal with the bus situation.
