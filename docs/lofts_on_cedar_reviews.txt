Westbrook University Off-Campus Housing: The Lofts on Cedar Reviews
Source: Westbrook Student Housing Forum & Rate My Landlord

---

Review by user cedar_lofts_resident_2023:
The Lofts on Cedar is the "luxury" option near Westbrook and honestly it lives up to the price tag, for better or worse. Rent is $1,400/month for a 1BR, $1,900 for a 2BR. You get: in-unit washer/dryer (huge), a gym that's actually decent, a rooftop deck, and a doorman on weekends. The apartments are modern, high ceilings, big windows. Management (Cedar Living LLC) is professional and responsive, maintenance requests usually handled within 24 hours. BUT: the walls are paper thin for a "luxury" building, you can hear everything in the hallway, and the rooftop is almost always crowded on weekends making it hard to actually enjoy. Parking included in rent (one spot).

Review by user wbu_grad_student_housing:
I chose The Lofts because I'm a grad student who needs to actually focus and it's worked out well. The building is quieter than dorms, people are mostly young professionals and grad students rather than undergrads looking to party. Soundproofing in the actual units is good, it's the hallways that are loud. The gym has free weights, treadmills, and a peloton which covers my needs. One thing: the lease requires renters insurance ($15-20/month through their preferred provider or you can get your own). This was not clearly disclosed when I signed, read the full lease. The location is a 15-minute walk to campus or 5 min by bus, not ideal for early morning classes.

Review by user lofts_on_cedar_review:
Moved out of The Lofts after one year and won't be going back. Not because of the apartment itself (which was nice) but because of the rent increase. They raised my rent $200/month at renewal. When I asked to negotiate they said take it or leave it. The initial 12-month lease had a clause allowing up to 10% rent increase at renewal, which I didn't notice when I signed. Know what you're agreeing to. The rooftop is genuinely amazing in summer, the gym is good, the in-unit laundry is a luxury I miss now. But $1,600 for a 1BR is just too much on a student budget.

Review by user westbrook_sophomore_transfer:
The Lofts is great if you can afford it. If you're on any kind of budget constraint, you will feel it every month. I split a 2BR with a roommate so it came to $950 each which is actually competitive with Maplewood for what you get. The kitchen is fully updated, dishwasher included. The main issue I had was noise from the bar district on Thursday-Saturday nights, the building is only a block from University Ave. Light sleepers beware. Management is excellent, they actually have a 24/7 emergency maintenance line that works. Internet included (150mbps) which is a nice perk.

Review by user cedar_resident_longtime:
Three things nobody tells you about The Lofts: (1) the parking garage has a tight clearance, larger trucks and SUVs do not fit — I had to park on the street for months before they found me a street-level spot. (2) The gym gets very crowded 6-9am and 5-8pm on weekdays, basically unusable at peak times. (3) The rooftop grill is first-come first-served and there's no reservation system, which leads to conflicts on nice days. These are minor things but worth knowing. The management team is genuinely the best I've dealt with — professional, quick, and fair about the deposit return process.
