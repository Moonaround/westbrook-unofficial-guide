Westbrook Off-Campus Housing: How to Read Your Lease
Source: Westbrook University Legal Aid Clinic (student-compiled guide)

---

WHAT TO READ BEFORE SIGNING ANY LEASE NEAR WESTBROOK

Most students sign leases without reading them fully. Here are the clauses that actually come up in disputes:

1. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
Look for: When is rent due? (Usually the 1st of the month) What's the grace period? (Typically 3-5 days in Westbrook) What's the late fee? (Common: $50-100 after grace period). Some Westbrook leases have automatic late fees with no grace period — look carefully.

2. LEASE TERM AND RENEWAL
Look for: What's the lease end date? Is it month-to-month after that, or does it terminate? What notice do you need to give if not renewing? (Usually 30-60 days). How much can rent increase at renewal? Some Westbrook leases are silent on this (landlord can raise to market rate); others cap it at a percentage.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Look for: How much is it? (Typically 1-2 months rent in Westbrook area). What can it be used for? (Should specify). How long does the landlord have to return it? (Westbrook law: 30 days). Is there a non-refundable portion (sometimes called "admin fee" or "move-in fee")? This money is gone regardless.

4. UTILITIES
Look for: Which utilities does the landlord pay? Which do you pay? This needs to be explicit. "Tenant pays utilities" is vague — make sure you know which ones. Common include: heat, electric, water/sewer, trash removal, internet.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
Look for: Who is responsible for what repairs? (Landlord must handle structural, major systems; tenants usually handle minor things). What's the process for requesting repairs? Is there a timeframe specified for response? Most Westbrook leases require repair requests in writing.

6. SUBLETTING AND GUESTS
Look for: Can you sublet? (Many Westbrook leases prohibit it entirely). What's the guest policy? (Some leases limit overnight guests to 7-14 consecutive days or a certain number of nights per month). This comes up in disputes more than students expect.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
Look for: What's the fee for breaking the lease early? (Ranges from 1-2 months rent in Westbrook area). Is there any scenario where you can break without penalty? (Usually: active military deployment, domestic violence situations, landlord breach of contract). Some leases have a "buy-out clause" with specific terms.

8. PETS
Look for: Are pets allowed? Which types/sizes? What's the pet deposit? (Non-refundable deposits are common, $300-500). Is there a monthly pet fee? Some Westbrook landlords allow pets with a deposit but charge a monthly fee ($25-50) on top.

9. ALTERATIONS
Look for: Can you paint? Hang pictures? Mount a TV? Install shelves? Most Westbrook leases allow minor changes (small nail holes) but prohibit significant alterations. Getting this in writing before you move in prevents deposit disputes.

10. ENTRY BY LANDLORD
Look for: How much notice must the landlord give before entering? (Westbrook law requires 24 hours except emergencies). Does the lease match this? If it says "landlord may enter at any time," that clause may not be enforceable under Westbrook city law, but it's better to have a compliant lease.

RED FLAGS IN WESTBROOK-AREA LEASES

These are clauses that have appeared in Westbrook area leases that students should push back on:

- "Tenant waives right to notice before entry" — unenforceable under Westbrook law; remove it
- "Automatic renewal for 12 months unless notice given 90 days before end of lease" — 90 days is a long notice period; negotiate to 30-60 days
- "Tenant responsible for all repairs up to $___" — fine if the amount is reasonable ($50-100); watch out if it's $500+
- "Non-refundable administrative fee of $___" in addition to security deposit — this money is gone; factor it into your true move-in cost
- "Landlord may charge for professional cleaning regardless of condition of unit at move-out" — this means you're paying for professional cleaning no matter what; at least it's transparent

Contact Westbrook Tenant Rights Organization (tenants@westbrookrights.org) if you have questions about specific lease clauses before signing.
