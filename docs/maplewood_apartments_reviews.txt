Westbrook University Off-Campus Housing: Maplewood Apartments Reviews
Source: Westbrook Student Housing Forum (r/WestbrookU)

---

Review by user throwaway_wbu_housing (posted Fall 2023):
Lived at Maplewood for two years. The rent is $850/month for a 1BR which is actually pretty fair for how close it is to campus — like a 10 minute walk to the main quad. Management (Greenfield Property Group) is hit or miss. They fixed our broken heater within 48 hours in January but took THREE MONTHS to patch a ceiling leak. Document everything with photos the day you move in or you will not get your deposit back. They charged my roommate $200 for "wall scuffs" that were already there. Laundry is in the basement, usually 4-5 washers available, costs $2.50/load. Parking is $75/month extra which adds up. Overall: decent location, mediocre management, fine if you document everything.

Review by user westbrook_junior_2024:
Maplewood is fine but not great. Pros: walking distance to the engineering building (about 8 min), cheap compared to The Lofts, usually quiet on weekdays. Cons: the walls are thin, you can hear neighbors clearly, and there was a mouse problem in Building C last year. They did eventually exterminate but it took them 2 weeks to respond to complaints. Internet is not included, I use Xfinity which runs about $60/month. No gym on site. The neighborhood is safe, I've never felt uncomfortable walking home at night. Lease starts August 1, not flexible on that date.

Review by user anonymous_wbu_student:
WARNING about Maplewood: check your lease for the "early termination fee." Mine was $1,500 which I found out after I needed to leave for a co-op in another city. They would not negotiate it down. Also be aware that subleasing is technically not allowed per the lease, though I've heard some people do it anyway at their own risk. The apartment itself was fine, no major issues, bathroom was updated recently. Heat and water included in rent which is a big deal. Just read the lease very carefully before signing.

Review by user wbu_off_campus_guide:
Maplewood Buildings A and B are newer (renovated 2019) and are noticeably nicer than Buildings C, D, and E which are older. If you're applying, specifically request A or B. The 2BR units are $1,200/month and split between two people that's pretty good. Common areas are okay, there's a courtyard that's nice in the fall. Mail gets stolen occasionally from the lobby mailboxes, use a PO box or Amazon locker for packages. Management office hours are Mon-Fri 9am-5pm, basically useless if you have an issue on a weekend.

Review by user wbu_transfer_student:
As a transfer student who moved from across the country I had to sign a lease sight-unseen. Maplewood was the most responsive to remote inquiries, they did a video tour for me which was helpful. The 1BR I got is small but functional. Noise from Route 9 traffic is noticeable in the front-facing units, ask for a unit facing the courtyard. Heat/water included is great, that's not common in the area. Closest grocery store is a 15 min walk (Trader Joe's) or 5 min drive. The bus route 12 stops right in front which connects to downtown Westbrook.
