Westbrook University Student Housing FAQ & Survival Guide
Source: Westbrook University Student Government Housing Committee (unofficial guide)

---

LEASE SIGNING TIPS

Q: When should I start looking for off-campus housing near Westbrook?
A: Start looking in October/November for the following August move-in. The best units near campus go fast. By February, most of the good 1BR and 2BR apartments within walking distance are already leased. If you're looking in the spring, expect to either settle for a less desirable unit or pay a premium for whatever is still available.

Q: What should I check before signing a lease near Westbrook?
A: The biggest things students miss: (1) Early termination fee — this can be $500 to $2,000+ and is almost never waived. (2) Subletting policy — many leases near Westbrook prohibit it. (3) Rent increase cap — some leases allow landlords to raise rent at renewal with minimal notice. (4) Utility responsibility — always clarify what's included. Heat is the big one in Westbrook winters. (5) Guest policy — some buildings have strict limits on overnight guests. (6) Pet policy and fees — even "pet-friendly" buildings usually charge $300-500 non-refundable pet deposits.

Q: What are the average rent prices near Westbrook University?
A: As of Fall 2023: Studio apartments within walking distance (under 15 min): $700-900/month. 1BR within walking distance: $850-1,400/month. 2BR within walking distance: $1,100-1,900/month. Units farther from campus (15-25 min walk or on a bus route): $600-800/month for a 1BR. Utilities typically add $60-120/month if not included.

DEPOSIT AND MOVE-OUT

Q: How do I protect my security deposit?
A: Three rules: (1) Take timestamped photos of every room, every wall, every appliance, every carpet stain on the day you move in. Email them to yourself and the landlord. (2) Do a formal walkthrough with the landlord at move-in and get any pre-existing damage in writing. (3) At move-out, do another walkthrough with the landlord present and get a written statement on the spot. Under Westbrook city law, landlords must return deposits within 30 days of move-out with an itemized list of any deductions.

Q: Can a landlord keep my deposit for normal wear and tear?
A: No. Under Westbrook tenant law, landlords cannot charge for "normal wear and tear" — this includes minor scuffs on walls, small nail holes, carpet wear from regular use, and faded paint. They CAN charge for large holes in walls, deep carpet stains, broken fixtures, missing items, and excessive cleaning. If you believe deductions are unfair, you can dispute them through the Westbrook Tenant Rights organization (tenants@westbrookrights.org).

UTILITIES AND INTERNET

Q: What internet providers serve the area around Westbrook University?
A: Xfinity is the main provider for most apartments within 2 miles of campus. Speeds typically range from 50mbps (basic, ~$50/month) to 400mbps (fast, ~$80/month). Some buildings have bulk agreements that include internet in rent. Spectrum serves some areas on the north side of campus. Many students split the cost with roommates to bring it to $20-30/person.

Q: What should I budget for utilities in a typical Westbrook apartment?
A: If utilities are not included: Heat (gas or electric) $40-80/month in warm months, $80-150/month November-March. Electric: $30-60/month. Water/sewer: often landlord-paid, but confirm. Internet: $50-80/month (or split). Total utility budget for a 1BR in winter: $150-250/month. Riverside Commons tenants report higher electric heating costs than gas-heated buildings.

NEIGHBORHOOD SAFETY AND TRANSPORTATION

Q: Which neighborhoods near Westbrook University are safest for students?
A: The blocks immediately surrounding campus (within 10 min walk) are generally very safe, with regular campus security patrols extending slightly off campus. The University Ave corridor is active and well-lit. The Cedar Street neighborhood (where The Lofts are located) is lively with bars and restaurants — generally safe but noisier at night. Riverside Commons area is residential and quiet but less foot traffic at night; the Route 7 bus is the main transit connection. Students are advised to walk in groups after midnight regardless of neighborhood.

Q: What bus routes serve off-campus student housing near Westbrook?
A: Route 12: runs along Main Street past Maplewood Apartments. Frequency: every 15 min during the day, every 30 min evenings until 1am. Route 7: runs to Riverside Commons. Frequency: every 20 min during the day, every 45 min evenings, stops at midnight. Route 3: University Ave loop, serves The Lofts on Cedar and downtown. Frequency: every 10 min during the day, every 20 min evenings until 2am. All routes are free with your Westbrook University student ID.
